Pallanguzhi

Traditional pit-and-pebble game.

Pallanguzhi is an ancient traditional board game of quick counting and strategy from South India. The goal is to capture more seeds than your opponent!

Rules:

  • Distribute your seeds counter-clockwise.
  • If the last seed falls in a pit, and the NEXT pit has seeds, pick them up and continue.
  • If the NEXT pit is empty, your turn ends.
  • You capture the seeds in the pit AFTER the empty one!
  • The player with the most seeds in their store wins.
