How to Play

  • Manage 4 stovetops simultaneously.
  • Tap a pot to instantly lower its heat by a small amount.
  • Drag/Stir across a pot to rapidly cool it down.
  • Beware of random events that can ruin your cooking!
  • If any pot hits 100%, it's GAME OVER!
